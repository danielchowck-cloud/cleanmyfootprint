Common questions

CleanMyFootprint is about clarity, control, and practical next steps.

Can you remove everything?

No. No honest service can promise that. We help identify and reduce unnecessary public exposure where possible.

Do you need my passwords?

No. The scan uses public-source information and details you provide. If a cleanup action requires account access, we tell you what to do yourself.

Is this legal?

The scan uses public information and client-provided details. Removal or correction requests are made only with client authorization.

What happens after the report?

You can keep it for awareness, request cleanup support, monitor key items, or improve controlled professional visibility.
